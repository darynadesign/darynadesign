Hey, I'm Daria! I used to design buildings —
now I design digital experiences. I strive to create emotionally resonant design that will
not only improve metrics but delight the user, making a lasting and memorable impact.

JUMP TO CASES

When I was a kid playing in a school band, I wanted to become a rockstar. At least that's what I wrote in my English essay in seventh grade. And then added, "In case that doesn't work out, my second best option would be a web designer."

Since then, I've taken a few detours —
a degree in project management, five years in architectural design, and a few wedding gigs — but I've come full circle to those childhood resolutions. I'm not quite a design rockstar (yet) but my twelve-year old self would be proud.
